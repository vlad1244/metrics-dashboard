Privacy Policy

Application: «тредс» (Threads App ID 1821069745800099). Last updated: November 5, 2026.

This application is a private tool used by its owner to manage his own Threads account (@vlad_lh): drafting and publishing posts, reading replies, and viewing post statistics. It is not offered to the public and is not used to collect data about other people for sale or advertising.

What data we access

  • Profile information of the connected Threads account (ID, username, name, biography).
  • Posts, replies and media published by the connected account.
  • Insights for the connected account and its posts (views, likes, replies, reposts, followers count).
  • Public posts returned by Threads keyword search and public profile information returned by the Threads API, used only to analyse which topics and formats perform well.

How we use it

Data is used only to prepare and publish content for the owner's account, reply to comments, and analyse performance. Nothing is published without the owner's manual approval. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising targeting.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer with access restricted to the owner. Analysis results are kept in private files. Data is kept only as long as needed for analysis and is deleted on request.

Data deletion

To delete your data or revoke access: open Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions and remove the app, or send a message to @vlad_lh on Threads saying «Delete my data». All data related to your account will be deleted within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.

Contact

Как используем

Только для подготовки и публикации контента владельца, ответов и анализа. Без ручного подтверждения владельца ничего не публикуется. Данные не продаются, не передаются третьим лицам и не используются для рекламы.

Хранение

Ключи доступа хранятся локально на компьютере владельца с закрытым доступом. Данные удаляются по запросу.

Удаление данных

Threads → Настройки → Аккаунт → Разрешения сайтов → удалить приложение, либо сообщение @vlad_lh в Threads: «Удалить мои данные». Удаление — в течение 30 дней с подтверждением.
